Table 18: Sample Impression Statements

  • Normal: “Normal MBFR confirms study normalcy… predicts a low-risk for major coronary-related events.”
  • Discordant (Normal Perfusion/Low MFR): “Despite normal myocardial perfusion, MBFR is abnormal, placing the patient in a higher risk category.”
  • Single Vessel: “Perfusion defect in a single territory with corresponding regional reduction in MBFR.”
  • Multivessel/Balanced: “Perfusion indicates single vessel disease, but MFR is globally reduced, raising concern for more extensive CAD.”
