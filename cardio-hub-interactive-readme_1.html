Calculators
Clinical echo and hemodynamic calculators with formula reference
LV / Filling pressures 3
LV diastolic pressure
LVdP = 1.06 × e^(0.148 × E/e′)
Estimates LV end-diastolic pressure from tissue Doppler ratio. Useful when invasive data are unavailable.
Ref: Nagueh SF et al. JACC 1997
LVOT gradient
ΔP = 4 × V²
Simplified Bernoulli equation applied to LVOT peak velocity. Also used for HOCM resting and provocable gradients.
Ref: 2011 AHA/ACC HOCM Guideline
ePAWP (E/e′ method)
ePAWP = 1.9 + (1.24 × E/e′)
Non-invasive estimate of pulmonary artery wedge pressure. Septal e′ preferred; lateral e′ formula differs.
Ref: Nagueh SF. JACC 2009; 2016 ASE Diastology Guidelines
Valvular disease 3
AVA — continuity equation
AVA = (CSA_LVOT × VTI_LVOT) / VTI_AV
Gold standard for aortic valve area. Indexed AVA (iAVA = AVA/BSA) adjusts for body size. Severe AS: AVA <1.0 cm², iAVA <0.6 cm²/m².
Ref: 2014 AHA/ACC Valvular Guidelines; 2021 update
MVA — pressure half-time
MVA = 220 / PHT
Empirical constant (220 ms·cm²) applied to mitral inflow deceleration slope. Less reliable post-commissurotomy.
Ref: Hatle L et al. Circulation 1979; 2014 AHA/ACC Valvular Guidelines
MR PISA / EROA
EROA = (2π × r² × Va) / Vpeak_MR
Effective regurgitant orifice area by proximal isovelocity surface area. RVol = EROA × VTI_MR. Severe MR: EROA ≥0.4 cm², RVol ≥60 mL (primary).
Ref: 2017 ASE MR Guidelines
Right heart / pulmonary hemodynamics 6
IVC-based RAP
RAP = 3 / 8 / 15 mmHg (by IVC + collapse)
IVC <2.1 cm + >50% collapse = 3 mmHg; IVC >2.1 cm + <50% collapse = 15 mmHg; indeterminate = 8 mmHg.
Ref: 2015 ASE RV Guidelines
RVSP
RVSP = 4 × V²_TR + RAP
Reflects PASP in the absence of RVOTO or pulmonic stenosis. Auto-populated from RAP.
Ref: 2015 ASE RV Guidelines; 2022 ESC PH Guidelines
Mean PAP — Chemla
mPAP = 0.61 × PASP + 2
Widely used regression-derived formula. 2022 ESC threshold: mPAP >20 mmHg defines PH (lowered from 25).
Ref: Chemla D. Circulation 2004; 2022 ESC PH Guidelines
Mean PAP — PR velocity
mPAP = 4 × V²_PR_early
Early diastolic PR velocity reflects mPAP directly. Independent of TR signal quality.
Ref: 2022 ESC PH Guidelines
PVR — Abbas formula
PVR = 10 × (V_TR / TVI_RVOT)
Non-invasive PVR estimate. ≥3 WU flags pre-capillary PH phenotype when mPAP is elevated.
Ref: Abbas AE. JACC 2003
TAPSE/PASP coupling
Coupling ratio = TAPSE (mm) / PASP (mmHg)
RV-PA coupling index. <0.55 mm/mmHg = uncoupled RV; TAPSE <17 mm flagged independently per 2022 ESC.
Ref: 2022 ESC PH Guidelines; Guazzi M. JACC 2013
Aortic dimensions 3
Ascending aorta — Yale model
Aortic index = diameter (cm) / BSA (m²)
Threshold ≥2.75 cm/m² suggests intervention consideration in selected patients per Yale criteria.
Ref: Elefteriades JA. Ann Thorac Surg 2002
Aortic root Z-score
Z = (measured − predicted) / SD
Sex-specific regression from Roman 1989. Predicted root diameter from BSA. Z ≥2 = dilated. Essential for Marfan/connective tissue evaluation.
Ref: Roman MJ et al. Am J Cardiol 1989
Indexed dimensions table
Index = segment (cm) / BSA (m²)
Reference table: annulus, sinuses, STJ, ascending aorta, descending. Normal ranges and aneurysm thresholds per 2022 AHA/ACC Aorta Guidelines.
Ref: 2022 AHA/ACC Aortic Disease Guidelines
ACS decision support 2
hs-Troponin — ESC 0/1h algorithm
Rule-out: <5 ng/L + Δ <2 at 1h
Roche Elecsys hs-cTnT. Rule-out (<5 ng/L at 0h + delta <2), rule-in (≥52 ng/L or Δ ≥5 at 1h), observe zone requires 3h sample.
Ref: 2020 ESC NSTE-ACS Guidelines; Roche Elecsys package insert
hs-Troponin — ESC 0/2h algorithm
Rule-out: <12 ng/L + Δ <3 at 2h
Alternative pathway when 1h sample not feasible. Zone highlighting with clinical action recommendations per ESC 2020.
Ref: 2020 ESC NSTE-ACS Guidelines
